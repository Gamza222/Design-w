A family with a child needed a calm, warm home that is easy to keep tidy and
pleasant to fill with life.

## The solution

A light base, natural wood and soft textiles. We planned plenty of open and closed
storage so daily life never disturbs the visual quiet.

The palette is warm and neutral — it doesn't age and is easy to refresh with details.
