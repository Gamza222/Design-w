## Composition and generous shapes

In the living room, the circular artwork draws the eye first. Its outline softens the straight edges of the walls, stairs and low dark console. A grey sofa and small table occupy the foreground, while the glass balustrade leaves the steps and the space behind them visible.

In the kitchen image, the island and tall cabinets form a calm horizontal composition. The dining table sits beside a large window. A round rug and curved chair backs distinguish this group visually without a solid partition.

## Colour, texture and light

Brown and charcoal tones connect the images. Wood grain sits alongside surfaces with stone-like veining. These are descriptions of their appearance, rather than a verified material specification.

Lighting behind the artwork, along the steps and above the kitchen worktop highlights individual elements. Daylight from the windows adds contrast and reveals the surface patterns. This visual direction can be a starting point for your own interior; proportions, circulation and lighting need to be checked against the actual room plan.
