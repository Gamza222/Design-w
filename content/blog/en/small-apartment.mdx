A compact apartment is not a limitation but an exercise in precision. Every
centimetre has to work, and the eye should move freely across the space.

## Five tricks

1. A single palette for floor and walls expands the boundaries.
2. Built-in storage instead of standalone furniture.
3. Mirrors opposite windows double the light.
4. Furniture on legs lets the floor "breathe" and feel larger.
5. One accent instead of many small details.

> Space is not the number of square metres, but the amount of air between objects.
