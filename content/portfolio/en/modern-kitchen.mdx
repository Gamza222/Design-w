## The island as a focal point

The kitchen island attracts most attention in both images. In the first, its form includes an open central section; in the second, the front appears more continuous. Expressive surface patterns contrast with the understated, plain cabinet fronts behind it.

Tall cabinets and built-in appliances form a single vertical plane. Glass-fronted shelves add depth while retaining the kitchen's overall geometry. Large side windows bring directional daylight that makes the surface details easier to see.

## Surface patterns and lines of light

The warm neutral palette draws attention to the veining on the island and splashback. An image can show their colour and scale, but it cannot establish an exact material, manufacturer or price.

Slim pendants, worktop lighting and ceiling lines repeat the horizontal composition. In the second image, lighting at the base visually separates the island from the floor. For your own kitchen, this idea should begin with checking circulation, appliance positions and service connections before choosing the finishes.
