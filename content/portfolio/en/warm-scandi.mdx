## Soft shapes and an open composition

In the first image, the light sofa separates the sitting area from the kitchen and dining table. Low round tables preserve the view through the room. The vertical texture of the island echoes the dark coffee table, while a curved pendant introduces another soft line above the dining table.

In the second image, a sofa also occupies the foreground, with a dining group visible beside the window. Framed wall details, glass pendants and a different cabinet geometry give this view its own character. These details are useful when comparing a simpler direction with a more decorative one.

## A light base with green accents

Cream and beige surfaces provide a background for muted greens in chairs, cushions and plants. Wood grain and visible textile texture bring variety to the neutral palette without a sharp colour contrast.

Curtains soften the daylight. Pendants above the table and lighting in the kitchen mark different parts of the space. When adapting this direction, lighting for cooking, conversation and relaxation deserves separate consideration beyond the appearance of the fittings.
