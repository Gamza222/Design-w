Light shapes how we perceive a space more than colour or furniture. Before choosing
wall tones, we study how the sun moves through the apartment over the day.

## Where to start

- Identify the orientation of each room.
- Don't block windows with bulky furniture.
- Use light fabrics that diffuse rather than block the light.

> A good interior looks different in the morning and the evening — and beautiful both times.

## Layered light

Complement natural light with artificial scenarios: ambient, task and accent. This
keeps the space alive even after sunset.
