The client wanted a studio without visual noise: an open space where the kitchen,
living room and work area flow into one another.

## The solution

We removed unnecessary partitions and set a single flooring material. Warm concrete,
wood and subtle clay accents make the minimalism cosy rather than cold.

Storage is hidden in floor-to-ceiling niches — the space stays clean.
