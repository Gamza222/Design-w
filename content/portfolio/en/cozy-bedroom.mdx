## A calm, symmetrical arrangement

The bed and broad upholstered headboard establish the centre of the room. Round bedside tables and two pendant lights are arranged symmetrically. Panels behind the headboard continue the vertical lines, while a low bench adds a horizontal shape in the foreground.

The bench has a soft, flowing outline. Its green base echoes the narrow inserts behind the bed. Repeating the accent brings colour to different parts of the image without dominating the calm base palette.

## Texture and soft light

Light beige, cream and grey dominate the image. Upholstery, bed linen, the rug and wall panels differ mainly through their textures. The image does not establish their exact materials or composition.

Daylight enters through the broad curtain on the right. Vertical lighting highlights the wall finish, while pendants mark the bedside areas. When developing a similar bedroom, the visual direction needs to be considered alongside bed height, clearances, storage and convenient lighting controls.
