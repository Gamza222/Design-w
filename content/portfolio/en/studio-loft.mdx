## Different areas within one space

The kitchen and living area are visible as one composition. A light corner sofa defines the sitting area, while a row of bar stools marks the kitchen boundary. Without a solid partition, the view continues through the room to the far wall.

A dark round coffee table softens the furniture's straight lines. The television wall on the right balances the kitchen at the back. A low console and large wall panels reinforce the horizontal rhythm.

## Graphic lines and a neutral palette

Black tracks and slim pendants stand out against the light ceiling. Lighting under the console, along the walls and above the worktop distinguishes individual zones. Beige and grey furniture leave the main contrast between light volumes and dark lines.

The image shows the relative positions of visible furniture, but gives no dimensions or complete plan. Adapting this direction to another room requires checking circulation, television sight lines, breakfast bar height and the arrangement of kitchen appliances.
