## Symmetry and a colourful focal point

The green sofa sits at the centre of a burgundy feature wall. Mirrored inserts and paired lights appear on either side. Two burgundy armchairs turn the composition towards the round coffee table and repeat the wall colour.

The artwork above the sofa, geometric mirrors and patterned rug give the interior a rich collection of details. A light ceiling and a broad window leave visual breathing space around the main furniture group. Part of a dining area on the right repeats the green accent.

## Reflections and decorative lighting

Gold-toned details appear in the table base, frames and light fittings. Transparent chandelier elements and mirrored surfaces reflect light, reinforcing the decorative character of the room.
